Baldwin Rotational Fuzzy Supplier Selection

Multi-attribute supplier rating from linguistic judgments, using Baldwin's rotational fuzzy set model. Implementation of Al-Humaidi's MADM method; ratings and importance weights are combined pointwise on the unit grid, aggregated by fuzzy weighted average, and rated by shortest centroid distance.

Supplier assessment — fixed factor hierarchy

The five factors and their sub-factors and sub-sub factors are fixed, as in the original program. Set the importance weight and rating in each dropdown; a factor weighted Not Important is excluded and its branch is greyed out.

Open implementation for the data availability statement — MIT license. Faithful port of the original Visual Basic program (Sustainability Indicator solution, Forms 1–20): identical membership functions, weighted-average aggregation, centroid, and both the original and the corrected distance conventions. Rating terms: Very/—/Fairly Positive, Undecided, Fairly/—/Very Negative. Weight terms: Very Important, Important, Fairly Important, Not Important (Very Important ≡ Very Positive ≡ y = x², etc.). “Undecided” is modeled as the constant membership y = 0.5; zero total weight yields a zero rating (the VB divide-by-10⁻¹⁴ guard is replaced by an exact zero).
